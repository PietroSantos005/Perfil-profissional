# 📚 Starting Point

I finished high school at a public institution at the age of 18. Since then, I have been building my professional path while balancing **work, education, and the development of new skills**.

Initially, I enrolled in a **Wood Industrial Engineering** program at a federal university. Later, due to changes in the academic calendar and the need to reassess my goals, I explored other educational paths.

I attended a two-year technical degree program at **PUCPR** for two semesters while simultaneously starting a **Computer Engineering** degree at Universidade Positivo.

Due to financial constraints, I eventually prioritized full-time work at **Positivo Tecnologia**, temporarily putting my studies on hold.

I am currently studying **Systems Analysis and Development** and continue to expand my knowledge, particularly in the technology sector.

Throughout my journey, I have developed experience with:

* 🌐 Website development
* 💻 Basic programming
* 📊 Spreadsheet creation and organization
* 🎨 Graphic design
* 📷 Photography

I have also gained professional experience in several different areas, including:

* Carpentry
* Customer service
* Kitchen work
* Bartending

* Technology

These experiences have helped me develop different skills and provided me with a broader understanding of the job market.
